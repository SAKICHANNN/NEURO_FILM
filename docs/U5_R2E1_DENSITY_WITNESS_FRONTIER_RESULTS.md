## Interpretation and boundary

This result shows that one original density-domain witness can be clearly
stylized, non-basic and severe-veto clean on the frozen development gold set.
It does not show that the witness is a measured film response, a named stock,
a latent stock mode, physically calibrated, independently preferred or ready
for production. The descriptive name is not an exposure, illuminant,
push/pull, process or scanner label.

Real-film operator fitting, current-pool learning, LSM and production
integration remain forbidden.
