# U6.P2X intrinsic Langmuir response photographic results

The second clean-room photographic realization is intrinsically density-bounded:
linear and Langmuir donors share one fixed sigmoid characteristic response and
no per-pixel post-hoc safety scale exists. Two 49-file runs are byte-identical
at `7d734754...9ff1`; the stable evidence ID is `37f849e7...45cfd`.

Boundedness alone does not make the mechanism useful or severe-safe. Median
Langmuir-versus-linear effect is only `.16768` Delta E76, and only 9/16 images
reach `.1`, failing the frozen `.25` and 14-image value gates. At the same time,
the worst isolated-excursion count is 126 against a required zero. Boundary,
style, non-basic, luma, chroma and gradient gates pass, but the three failures
close the family before visual review.

No slope, midpoint, coupling, donor, exposure encoding or threshold rescue is
allowed. U6.P2V remains synthetic mechanism evidence only. Further photographic
interimage work now requires independently measured response data rather than
another heuristic equation family; nothing here is measured sensitometry,
chemistry, stock/process/scanner calibration or product evidence.
