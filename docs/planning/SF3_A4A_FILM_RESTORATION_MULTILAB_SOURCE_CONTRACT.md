# SF3.A4A — Multi-lab colour-film restoration source readiness

## Role

This is a prospective zero-media source-readiness audit of the 2026 paper
`Subjectivity in color film restoration: A multi-lab comparison` (DOI
`10.1016/j.daach.2026.e00538`). The official public record describes six
laboratories restoring the same 1972 colour film from a common original
camera negative and positive-print reference, with an independent restoration
supervised by the film's director of photography retained as an external
reference. The evaluation topology is materially useful for the auxiliary A5
operator/restoration-uncertainty lane.

The source does not identify Velvia 50, Portra 400 or Ektar 100 and therefore
cannot count toward target-stock evidence, candidate-three admission or a
stock-calibrated product claim.

## Frozen sources and operations

1. Read only selected bibliographic metadata from the official Elsevier API,
   Crossref and an exact-title DataCite query.
2. Bind DOI, PII, title, journal, publication date, authors, open-access state,
   Crossref relation state and the exact-title DataCite result after removing
   mutable indexing timestamps and response links.
3. Bind the public article topology as a prospectively recorded official-page
   fact: six laboratories, three scenes, 24 frames per scene, common original
   camera negative, positive-print reference and a held independent
   director-of-photography restoration.
4. Make zero requests for article media, PDF bytes, the source negative,
   positive print, laboratory versions, director-of-photography version,
   frames, thumbnails or pixels.

## Admission gates

A later data leaf requires a public observation payload, exact manifest and
checksums, an explicit observation-data licence compatible with fitting and
derived parameters, exact laboratory and scanner/workflow identities,
prospective development groups, a sealed confirmation group and an exact film
stock identity. Article copyright and text/data-mining terms do not grant
rights to unexposed observation assets.

## Stop rules

- Do not infer a stock identity from the film title, year, country or article.
- Do not treat the article's six laboratory outputs as public data.
- Do not fetch PDF, figures, frames, negative, print or restoration media.
- Do not fit, render, score or tune around absent data.
- Failure closes this public source route only. It is not a quality conclusion
  about the laboratories, the restoration or the historical film.

## Claim ceiling

At most this leaf can establish a current official bibliographic identity and
a materially useful multi-lab restoration topology for a future auxiliary A5
study. It cannot establish public reusable media, observed group identities,
film-stock response, any target-stock evidence, candidate-three admission,
calibration, training eligibility or product capability.
