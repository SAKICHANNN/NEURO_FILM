## Candidate

For row-vector RGB `x`, rotation matrix `Q`, projected-cube minima `l` and
ranges `r`:

```text
t = x Q
u = (t - l) / r
v_i = C_i(u_i)
t' = l + r v
y = t' Q^T
```

`Q` is generated by Rodrigues' formula from a bounded rotation vector:

```text
w = 0.45 * tanh(||z||) * z / ||z||
```

with an exact zero branch. It is always right-handed `SO(3)`, has determinant
one and condition number one. `l` and `r` come from projecting all eight
corners of the complete RGB cube; image extrema never enter the operator.

Each `C_i` is a degree-six Bernstein curve with exact endpoints and six
strictly positive increments of at least `0.02`. The operator has 21 raw and
18 effective parameters:

- three rotation parameters;
- 18 curve logits / 15 effective curve degrees of freedom.

The full-strength inverse is not a multidimensional fixed-point solve:

1. multiply output by `Q`;
2. undo the fixed cube normalization;
3. invert each strictly monotone scalar Bernstein curve with exactly 64
   bisection iterations;
4. multiply by `Q^T`.

Strength is the exact line from identity to the full output. No clipping,
projection or content-dependent range repair is allowed.

## Targets and controls

Fit identity and the two frozen non-identity targets independently.

Controls use the same target points and deterministic float64 CPU protocol:

1. three global degree-six monotone RGB curves, 15 effective parameters;
2. the same curves followed by a nonnegative row-stochastic matrix with
   maximum mix `0.35`, 22 effective parameters;
3. stationary cube-diffeomorphic K3 flow, 81 raw parameters, 24-step RK4.

The candidate must beat both compact controls and remain within `1.20x` of K3.
This prevents a trivial same-family recovery claim and tests whether rotation
buys useful capacity rather than merely replacing a positive matrix.

## Automatic gates

Every non-identity target must jointly pass:

- candidate RMSE at most `0.015`;
- at least 20% improvement over global curves;
- at least 10% improvement over positive-matrix curves;
- RMSE no more than `1.20x` stationary K3;
- cube range within numerical rails `[-1e-12,1+1e-12]`, without clamp;
- minimum determinant `0.02`, maximum Jacobian norm `8`;
- inverse roundtrip at most `2e-8`;
- rotation orthogonality/determinant error at most `1e-12`, angle at most
  `0.45`;
- curve increments at least `0.02`;
- neutral channel spread at most `0.03`;
- hue-neighbour output jump at most `1e-5`;
- nondecreasing, smoothly bounded red-ramp luma;
- collinear, ordered strength path;
- exact identity, serialization and partition replay.

Two fresh processes must produce byte-identical canonical reports. Identity or
target prerequisite failure invalidates the run rather than weakening a
candidate gate.
