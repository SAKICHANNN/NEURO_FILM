`7a6d492476ae97458d1a8948d6fe37a9c309c79efb6aa84813fc8f65a6044ef6`.

Each process revalidates the archive, AJ0B2 manifest and every member identity.

decode failure invalidates the complete run; it cannot be laundered into an
individual candidate veto. A metric-gate failure vetoes only that candidate
and does not skip or replace it. ZIP members are read in memory one at a time
and are never extracted to disk.

Paths and their hashes are identities only. Filename, directory, maker,
stock-like wording, Push/Pull-like suffixes and all other text are forbidden
as metrics, grouping inputs, ranking inputs or truth.

## Individual eligibility

Every C0B threshold is unchanged. A candidate is eligible only if it:

1. is finite and in `[0,1]` without output clamp;
2. passes every endpoint, Jacobian, neutral reversal, gradient and native-code
   structural gate;
3. has style median Delta E76 at least `3.0`;
4. has matched-basic residual median Delta E76 at least `1.5`.

Metric failures are recorded as structural veto, style-ineligible or
basic-explainable. Source identity/decode failure is a fatal evidence failure,
not a candidate state. There is no smoothing, projection, clamping, strength
reduction, threshold weakening or replacement.

## Normalized safety margin

Safety margin is a tie-break only after style and non-basic strength. For
every upper-bound safety metric it is `clip((limit-value)/limit,0,1)`.
For minimum Jacobian determinant it is
`clip((value-(-0.05))/0.05,0,1)`. The aggregate is the minimum of the ten
terms listed in the config. Finite/range are hard vetoes, not margins.

## Duplicate and strength collapse

Only eligible candidates enter components:

1. exact native-table SHA-256 joins exact duplicates;
2. otherwise positive-scale strength equivalence uses the unchanged C0B
   effect signature and gates: cosine at least `.995`, explained energy at
   least `.99`, bilateral normalized residual at most `.10`;
3. build a graph over exact groups using the strength relation;
4. collapse a strength-connected component only when every pair in it is an
   edge (complete-link/clique);
5. if a connected component is not a clique, retain its exact groups and
   record a non-transitive strength ambiguity instead of chain-merging;
6. results must be identical under parent, reverse-parent and
   candidate-identity-hash orders.

Within each component the representative is chosen lexicographically by:

1. highest style median Delta E76;
2. highest matched-basic residual median Delta E76;
3. highest normalized safety margin;
4. lowest native-table SHA-256;
5. lowest path-identity SHA-256.

This deliberately chooses the strongest eligible point on a strength path,
consistent with the product objective. Text never breaks a tie.

## Cap-12 diversity frontier

Residual signature is `Lab(output)-Lab(matched-basic output)` on the frozen
17-cube. Distance is median per-sample Delta E76.

Selection is deterministic:

1. seed with the representative ranking above;
2. repeatedly choose the remaining representative with the largest minimum
   distance to the selected set;
3. break ties by style, non-basic residual, safety margin, native-table hash
   and path-identity hash in that order;
4. stop at 12 or when the best remaining minimum distance is below `1.0`.

All pairwise distances and tie fields are recorded. One selected
representative may open design of a later photographic evaluation contract,
but only as a singleton challenger without a bank-diversity claim. Two or
more support a structurally distinct frontier claim. Zero selected closes
this external-bank challenger without rescue.
