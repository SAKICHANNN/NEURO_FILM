# U4.1A Three-Stock Gold Severe-Adjudication Contract

## Product question

Do the current three explicit K=1 film-inspired / Look Approximation product
looks preserve content without a confirmed severe artifact on every member of
the already-frozen nine-input U4.1 gold set, including direct ingress of the
original FilmSet face file?

This closes a product content-safety evidence gap. It is not a stock-response,
target-film, preference, training, source-search or new algorithm leaf.

- Select exactly the nine existing `gold` rows: `01`, `05`, `08`, `09`, `11`,

  `data/raw/filmset/FilmSet/train/input/DSCF01200 iso1600.png`, not the archived

- Render exactly `velvia_50`, `portra_400`, `ektar_100` at amount `1.0` through
  the unchanged public product renderer and
  `safe_rich_product_v1`. Output is PNG16 plus strict replayable recipe.
- The renderer, profile, stock statistics, guardrails, product schema, source
  geometry and all thresholds are frozen before output generation.

## Automatic and visual gates

For every one of the 27 source/look cells:

1. input identity and geometry are exact; output is finite, geometry-preserving
   and strict-replay byte exact;
2. new exact-boundary fraction is zero and existing content-safety metrics are
   recorded without replacing visual adjudication;
3. one source/output overview plus four deterministic 256x256 exact-pixel
   crops is materialized from the frozen source and output bytes. The crop
   score maps are fixed as flat-region added high frequency, edge-direction
   disagreement, edge-gain tail and encoded-RGB absolute delta;
4. three complete autonomous review passes use fixed arm rotations and inspect
   dirty/blotchy colour, isolated chroma impulses, banding/posterization,
   objectionable halos and face/text/object/fine-structure loss;
5. every pass records one non-uncertain verdict per cell. Any confirmed severe
   verdict or unresolved cell fails the corresponding arm and U4.1A overall.

The known ID11 discriminating source is mandatory. Automatic metrics cannot
hide, downgrade or overrule a visual severe verdict. Review material may not be
re-rendered, cropped differently or substituted after the first verdict.

## Reproducibility and stop rule

- Forward/reverse source enumeration must yield one exact scientific payload,
  the same 27 output-byte identities, the same 27 recipe semantic identities
  after redacting only the contract-required absolute input/output paths, and
  the same review material. Each run still requires byte-exact strict replay
  against its unredacted recipe.
- Formal render and review roles are create-only under the repo-relative
  P-backed output root; source bytes are immutable, network reads are zero and
  owned scratch is removed.
- Any source/hash/coverage/replay/order/visual gate failure closes this exact
  current-product gold adjudication. Do not weaken amount, remove ID11, select
  crops after review, replace the source, change the renderer or open an
  adjacent aesthetic-preference rescue.

## Claim ceiling

A pass would establish only autonomous severe-artifact evidence for the current
three deterministic Look Approximation choices on nine frozen internal gold
inputs. It would not establish calibrated or physical film response,
stock distinguishability, target-film closeness, preference, universal safety,
commercial rights for FilmSet, public release or product value.
