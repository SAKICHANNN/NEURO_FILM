# U8.2B — Private Look Approximation fact cards contract

## Purpose

Generate one deterministic, machine-readable fact-card bundle for the exact
current private product runtime. The bundle must describe the renderer, its
data/model status, every authoritative product Look, the blocked generic B&W
row, supported product boundaries, and unresolved release gaps.

This is factual release preparation. It does not choose a root licence,
authorize public distribution, validate a named film stock, or change pixels,
profiles, recipes, launchers, input formats, or availability.

## Required cards

1. `product`: private Windows repository-bound runtime, deterministic explicit
   colour operator, supported CLI/desktop entrypoints, and claim ceiling.
2. `data`: no calibrated stock-response training corpus and no permission to
   infer authenticity from manufacturer data sheets, unpaired scans, proxies,
   presets, or current source audits.
3. `model`: no learned final-RGB generator; the product path is deterministic
   and bounded, with learned/research branches excluded from this claim.
4. `profiles`: exactly the authoritative catalog rows in catalog order. The
   three colour rows remain available Look Approximations; generic B&W remains
   `blocked_severe_artifact` with its exact evidence binding and reason.
5. `release`: `public_release=false`, root licence unresolved, calibrated
   stock evidence absent, signing/installer certification/closed beta pending,
   and the exact installed runtime receipt identity.

No timestamp, host path, username, environment variable, random identifier,
or prose inferred outside the frozen inputs may enter the canonical bundle.

## Gates

- receipt/profile/evidence identities exact;
- runtime claim and repository binding exact;
- root `LICENSE` absent and represented only as an unresolved release gap;
- product catalog complete, ordered, unique, and unchanged;
- every available colour Look is explicitly `film-inspired` and
  `look-approximation`, never calibrated or physical-film evidence;
- blocked B&W remains blocked with exact reason/evidence;
- data and model limitations explicit;
- canonical JSON deterministic across repeated and reversed input mappings;
- malformed, missing, duplicated, reordered, overclaimed, or stale inputs
  reject;
- create-only publication preserves existing and late foreign destinations;
- owned stage residue zero after rejection.

## Stop rule

Any identity, completeness, determinism, claim, availability, or publication
failure closes U8.2B. Do not infer a licence, hide a blocked row, call an
unpaired/proxy source calibrated evidence, relax exact identity, rebuild the
runtime, or change a product asset as rescue.

## Claim ceiling

`PASS_PRIVATE_LOOK_APPROXIMATION_FACT_CARDS` means only that the exact private
runtime has deterministic factual product/data/model/profile/release cards.
It is not legal clearance, a public release, calibrated stock response,
physical-film reproduction, population preference, universal safety,
installer certification, signing, or cross-platform parity.
