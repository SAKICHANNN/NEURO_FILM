# U6.P2N silver-retention density contract

P2N adds a separate process branch for colour material in which a bounded
fraction of neutral metallic-silver density remains alongside developed dye
density. Normal colour bleach/fix remains the exact zero-retention control.
The operator runs before transmittance and scanning; it is not an encoded-RGB
noise layer, contrast preset, or saturation slider.

The fixed `0.35` retention fraction and luma-derived density weights are
hypothesis-only generic values. They are not inferred from photographs and do
not identify bleach bypass, a stock, a lab, or a process session. P2N first
tests only mathematical composition, domain safety, deterministic partitioning
and identity. A pass can open a separately frozen photographic severe/style
test, not product integration.

The executable contract is
`configs/u6_p2n_silver_retention_density_v1.json`. Kodak's
<https://www.kodak.com/en/motion/page/processing-techniques/> documents the
qualitative retained-silver effect; it does not calibrate this profile.
