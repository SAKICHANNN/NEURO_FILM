# U5.R2AI1 dual-champion independent-confirmation contract

Status: **frozen before any confirmation render**

Node: `ULT > U5 > U5.R2 > U5.R2AI1`

## Question

Does the single R2AI0-retained deterministic composition preserve material
non-basic style, complementary value over both parents and severe-artifact
safety on the independently frozen R2AI1S digital-photo population?

This is not a search. The only candidate is:

`density_then_anchor__density_s50`

It uses density strength `0.50`, density before anchor, and one final
four-code RGB8 output margin. No order, strength, margin, resize, crop,
per-image choice, fitting or effect may change.

## Exact independent population

R2AI1 binds the R2AI1S contract, decision, manifest, automatic report and
autonomous source review by SHA-256. The eligible population is exactly the
17 IDs in the configuration, across nine camera makes.

`fujifilm_s2pro` remains in the source audit but is excluded because visual
review found a Kodak target that violates the selection rule frozen before
decoding. It is not replaced. No further row may be removed after an operator
output is seen.

## Fixed outputs

Render exactly three outputs for every eligible source:

1. the standalone `anchor56_chroma_margin4_challenger`;
2. the standalone `cyan_shadow_warm_highlight_like__s50`;
3. the retained density-then-anchor composition.

The identity source is a metric control, not a fourth style candidate.
Every input and decoded preview hash must be rechecked. Outputs remain the
same dimensions, use no spatial resampling or effects, retain float
intermediates, and quantize once to RGB8 PNG.

Two independent processes must produce byte-identical manifests and automatic
reports before visual review.

## Automatic gates

The confirmation thresholds are fixed from R2AI0 development evidence before
any R2AI1 output exists.

### Development retention

The retained candidate must keep at least 80% of its development-wide median:

- median style Delta E76 >= `10.9333706362`;
- median non-basic residual Delta E76 >= `9.9209796165`.

To reject uncontrolled OOD overcorrection, confirmation style must also stay
inside 125% of the development envelope:

- p95 style Delta E76 <= `30.2092973105`;
- maximum per-image style Delta E76 <= `31.4746061144`.

### Complementary value

For both style and non-basic residual:

- candidate median gain over the stronger parent must be >= `0.25` Delta E76;
- candidate must beat both parents on at least 10 of 17 images;
- camera-make median must beat both parents for at least six of nine makes.

The non-basic metric removes the frozen joint EV, white-balance, contrast,
saturation and affine explanation. It is the guard against calling a merely
stronger saturation/contrast treatment a successful composition.

### Output safety

- worst new hard clipping per candidate output <= `0.5%`;
- every output is finite, hash-valid, RGB, source-sized and exactly replayed;
- no automatic failure may be rescued by changing a threshold or source row.

## Autonomous visual gates

Only a complete automatic pass opens three blind rounds. Each sheet shows the
fixed source as context and randomizes only the three output identities. All
17 rows appear in every round. Rankings must be written before the private
mapping is read.

The candidate must rank ahead of both parents in at least two of three rounds.
This is autonomous visual evidence, not a new owner vote or population
preference result.

After reveal, all 17 candidate outputs require full-resolution source/output
review. Any confirmed severe face/text/object corruption, geometry break,
posterization, banding, colour block, seam, unintended clipping or unstable
colour artifact vetoes the result. People/skin, text, architecture, foliage,
water/specular detail, smooth sky/gradient, snow highlight/deep shadow and
tungsten scenes are explicit regression categories.

## Branches and claims

- repeat failure: close and diagnose implementation drift;
- automatic failure: retain only the R2AI0 development result;
- blind or severe failure: veto independent confirmation;
- complete pass: retain one independently confirmed B0 challenger and open
  only a separate product-readiness contract.

No branch permits threshold, order, strength, margin, resize, row-selection or
aesthetic rescue. A pass does not change the production default and does not
establish owner or population preference, universal safety, real-film
operator identification, stock response, calibration, latent modes,
authenticity or production readiness.
