# U5.R2AI0 Dual-Champion Global Composition Contract

Date frozen: 2026-07-28  
Node: `ULT > U5 > U5.R2 > U5.R2AI0`  
Status: **frozen before implementation or output inspection**

## Question

Can one fixed sequential composition of the two independently severe-clean
global B0 champions create materially stronger, non-basic film-inspired colour
while preserving the hard severe-artifact budget?

This is a deterministic product-style frontier, not stock learning. It does
not infer an operator from photographs, use current film pixels, train a model
or reopen any failed reference-only branch.

## Parent evidence and readiness

`anchor56_chroma_margin4_challenger` is the safe descendant of the user's
preferred `53/55/56` direction. It has strong cyan/blue-shadow versus warm
highlight separation, zero new clipping and no confirmed severe gold failure.

`cyan_shadow_warm_highlight_like__s50` is the retained clean-room
density-domain champion. It reaches gold style `13.4944`, non-basic residual
`7.3946`, zero new gold/stress clipping and zero severe failure in 27
full-resolution shortlist renders.

The mechanisms are independently implemented and visually complementary.
Neither result tests their sequential composition. Both source configs,
decisions, manifests, renderers and the 41-image frozen set are hash-pinned in
`configs/u5_r2ai0_dual_champion_global_composition_v1.json`.

## Fixed candidate bank

Exactly six candidates are allowed:

- `anchor_then_density` at density strength `0.20`, `0.35`, `0.50`;
- `density_then_anchor` at density strength `0.20`, `0.35`, `0.50`.

Every candidate:

- uses the exact parent anchor parameters;
- uses the exact parent density witness;
- has no grain, effects, local adaptation, fitting or per-image choice;
- retains a floating-point intermediate with no intermediate encoding;
- applies the inherited four-code output margin once at the final boundary;
- writes one final RGB8 PNG only.

The two parent champions are comparators, not new candidates. No other order,
strength, blend, margin, curve, LUT, projection or post-result parameter is
allowed.

## Automatic gate

Render all six candidates on all nine gold and 32 stress inputs twice. Source
hash, dimensions, RGB decode, completeness, finite state and byte-identical
repeat manifests are conjunctive.

A candidate survives only when:

- gold median style Delta E76 is at least `13.9944`;
- gold median matched-basic residual Delta E76 is at least `7.8946`;
- worst-gold new hard-clipping fraction is at most `0.5%`;
- every output is finite, decode-clean and dimension-preserving.

The first two floors require a fixed `0.5 Delta E76` material gain over the
stronger E1 parent on both views. Worst stress clipping is reported rather
than averaged into the gold gate.

## Visual veto and selection

Only automatic survivors enter three independently shuffled anonymous gold
rounds against both parents. A retained composition must:

- win at least two of three rounds over both parents;
- show visibly stronger or more coherent style, not only greater saturation;
- have zero confirmed severe failure on full-resolution review of all nine
  gold images;
- pass explicit ID11 red-speckle/posterization review;
- preserve face/skin, text, fine texture, smooth gradients, highlights,
  shadows and geometry.

One severe failure vetoes that candidate. If several candidates pass, select
the lower density strength; remaining ties prefer `anchor_then_density`.
Autonomous review is not owner, independent-human or population preference.

## Branches

- **No automatic survivor:** close the composition hypothesis.
- **Visual or severe failure:** veto; close if no candidate remains.
- **Pass:** retain at most one B0 challenger. Do not change the product
  default or claim stock truth.

Failure cannot be rescued by expanding the strength grid, reversing a
candidate after results, changing the margin, weakening gates, fitting a
post-transform, adding ML, or choosing per image.

## Claim ceiling

At most, this establishes autonomous B0 evidence for a fixed composition of
two deterministic film-inspired global Look Approximation operators. It
cannot establish a digital-to-film operator, stock response, latent mode,
calibration, authenticity, owner/population preference or production
promotion.

## Definition of done

U5.R2AI0 closes only after:

1. isolated implementation and tests;
2. two complete repeat renders and automatic reports;
3. any permitted anonymous/full-resolution review;
4. machine-readable decision and evidence report;
5. targeted plus complete CPU verification;
6. propagation, scoped commit and push.
