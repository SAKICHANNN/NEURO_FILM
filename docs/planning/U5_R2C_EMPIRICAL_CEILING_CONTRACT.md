# U5.R2C fixed-bank empirical-ceiling contract

Date: 2026-07-23  
Node: `ULT > U5 > U5.R2 > U5.R2C`  
Status: **frozen before any B1 scene acquisition, participant contact or B1 outcome**

## Question

Does scene-wise selection from the already frozen U5.R2B bank have enough
independent product value to justify adaptive routing, compared with the
strongest qualified global policy?

This is an empirical fixed-bank ceiling, not a universal Oracle. It can reject
the need for routing. It cannot establish film-stock authenticity, a
digital-to-film operator, calibrated severe risk or population preference.

## Parent evidence and branch

U5.R2B retains `anchor56_chroma_margin4_challenger` as one qualified B0 global
Look Approximation challenger. Anchor09 passes automatic metrics but fails the
full-resolution ID11 severe-artifact veto. Raw 01/53/55/56 fail the global
clipping gate and safe-rich is materially bland.

U5.R2C therefore freezes:

1. the complete seven-policy bank and identity fallback;
2. a disjoint selection-panel/evaluation-panel protocol;
3. all-scene tie-score accounting;
4. a transparent, non-binding annotation workload;
5. the stop branch if the empirical ceiling cannot beat the global policy.

The current A0/B0 images and outcomes are never B1 data.

## Fixed bank and comparator

The candidate budget is `K=7`, exactly the U5.R2B primary bank:

1. `bland_safe_rich_control`;
2. `anchor01_color_only`;
3. `anchor09_color_only`;
4. `anchor53_color_only`;
5. `anchor55_color_only`;
6. `anchor56_color_only`;
7. `anchor56_chroma_margin4_challenger`.

Identity is a fail-closed fallback and is not counted in `K`. No candidate,
parameter, strength, renderer, export rule or budget may be changed after the
B1 manifest opens.

The frozen eligible global comparator is
`anchor56_chroma_margin4_challenger`. If later evidence invalidates this
comparator before B1 opens, U5.R2C must be re-frozen as a new version; it must
not be silently substituted.

## B1 data DoR

B1 cannot open until a separately committed manifest proves:

- rights-cleared pixel use and exact source/license snapshots;
- independence from A0, A1 and B0 by parent scene, source/uploader, camera,
  roll when known, exact hash and perceptual hash;
- one parent scene per provenance cluster for the primary analysis, or a
  prevalidated clustered design;
- frozen deployment-mixture strata and sampling probabilities;
- adequate faces/skin, text, smooth gradients, saturated highlights, foliage,
  architecture, low light, mixed light and neutral scenes;
- one identical renderer/export path for every bank member and comparator;
- no use of current real-film pools for fitting, training or LSM.

The existing 9 gold + 32 stress set is A0/B0 development evidence and is
ineligible. Downloading or rendering a B1 pixel set is not authorized by this
contract.

## Panel isolation

Selection panel `S` and evaluation panel `E` must have disjoint participant
identities, recruitment batches and randomized presentation seeds. Neither
panel may see candidate IDs, prior metrics, U5.R2B outcomes or the other
panel's labels.

AI/VLM review may remain a separate secondary reproducibility channel. It
cannot populate either human panel or support a cross-rater/population claim.

### Selection panel S

For every scene and every one of the seven candidates:

1. collect three initial severe-artifact labels under the frozen U5.R1A
   `3+3` rule;
2. collect three look-adherence and style-strength labels;
3. reject a candidate for that scene if severity resolves severe/unresolved,
   look adherence does not have at least two `pass` votes, or any required
   record is missing;
4. each ranking rater ranks all remaining candidates with ties allowed;
5. select the lowest mean rank; break exact ties by higher median style
   strength, then by the frozen candidate order above;
6. if no candidate remains, deploy identity.

All seven candidate outcomes remain in the annotation and cost ledger even
when rejected. A globally rejected U5.R2B policy may be locally eligible only
if the new scene's independent labels pass; its old A0/B0 outcome is never
copied forward.

### Evaluation panel E

Panel E receives only the complete deployed output and the frozen global
comparator, left/right randomized:

- three independent keep-preference ratings with ties allowed;
- three independent deployed-output severe-artifact ratings under the same
  conservative `3+3` rule;
- three independent deployed-output look-adherence ratings.

When the selected output is byte-identical to the comparator, the preference
outcome is deterministically a tie, but the scene remains in every denominator.
No selective dropping, candidate-only denominator or “qualified scenes only”
efficacy endpoint is allowed.

## Endpoints and decision

The primary efficacy endpoint is the all-scene deployed-output tie score

`W = P(win) + 0.5 * P(tie)`

against the global comparator. Scene is the independent unit; reviewer
dependence is modeled or clustered. The planning target remains a one-sided
95% lower bound above `0.55`, but the binding estimator, sample size and
critical value remain unknown until the label-error, cluster and tie-model
pilot is complete.

Safety and look are required constraints, not efficacy substitutes:

- unresolved/missing severity counts severe;
- every identity fallback remains in overall-risk and efficacy denominators;
- look failure cannot be reclassified as safety;
- style strength cannot overrule a severe veto;
- stress strata are reported separately from the deployment-mixture endpoint.

Branch:

- supported all-scene gain with acceptable safety/look opens only a separately
  frozen U5.R3 scorer experiment;
- no supported gain, infeasible annotation budget or invalid data closes
  adaptive routing and retains the global challenger/safe-rich fallback;
- inconclusive evidence does not open R3;
- no branch changes the production default without later hidden/product gates.

## Non-binding workload model

Let:

- `N` be independent B1 scenes;
- `K=7`;
- `r_s=r_l=r_rank=r_pref=r_eval_s=r_eval_l=3`.

Base judgement count:

`N * K * (r_s + r_l) + N * (r_rank + r_pref + r_eval_s + r_eval_l)`

This equals `54N`: 5,400 at `N=100`, 16,200 at `N=300`, and 32,400 at
`N=600`. It excludes qualification, sentinels, repeats, training, breaks and
senior severity adjudication. If every candidate and deployed output escalated
to three senior severity judgements, the conservative upper workload becomes
`78N`: 7,800, 23,400 and 46,800 respectively.

These are annotation judgements, not independent scenes or statistical power.
The binding sample size remains `unknown`. Correlated crops, extra candidates
or repeated ratings cannot replace independent scenes.

## Allowed implementation leaf

Before any human or B1 data work, local code may:

- validate the frozen bank/comparator and panel isolation;
- calculate transparent base/escalation workload scenarios;
- validate that identity fallback and all-scene denominators are present;
- reject A0/B0 lineage in a proposed B1 manifest;
- emit a preregistration worksheet with binding power fields still null.

It may not acquire pixels, contact or recruit participants, spend money,
train/fit a model, inspect B1 outcomes, modify rendering, or open R3.

- the worksheet reports `binding_sample_size=null`;
