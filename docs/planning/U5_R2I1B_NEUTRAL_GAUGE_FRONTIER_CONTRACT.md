# U5.R2I1B Neutral-Gauge Frontier Contract

**Frozen:** 2026-07-24  
**Node:** `ULT > U5 > U5.R2 > U5.R2I1B`  
**Split:** B0 autonomous development

## Question

Does the numerically passed, data-independent U5.R2I1 neutral gauge turn the
green but strong U2.2B control into a visually competitive, non-basic and
severe-clean Look Approximation?

## Fixed design

Reuse the exact U5.R2I0 9-gold/32-stress set, inherited metrics/gates,
comparators and strength values. The sole architectural change is insertion of
the frozen 1,025-knot inverse-neutral gauge before the same explicit linear-RGB
identity blend. The gauge cannot read an image statistic and no operator
parameter may change.

Strengths remain one continuous path, not modes. At most two automatic
survivors advance by non-basic residual, style and lower-strength tie break.
Three blind rounds include safe-rich, anchor 56 and the R2E1 retained density
challenger. All nine gold images and full-resolution ID11 review are required.

## Gates and branches

Automatic gates are unchanged: gold style at least `7.0`, non-basic residual at
least `4.9`, worst-gold new clipping at most `0.5%`.

- no survivor closes the gauged route;
- any severe artifact rejects that strength regardless of style;
- a stable cast or three-round comparator loss closes visual value without
  calling the clean output a glitch;
- a severe-clean visual winner may be retained only as a B0 uncalibrated
  challenger for later fixed comparison, not production or stock truth.

Two render manifests and repeated automatic reports must be byte-identical.
No result-driven knot/strength/curve change, film-pixel fitting, training, LSM,
generative RGB, profile/default integration, stock response, calibration,
authenticity or owner/population preference claim is allowed.
