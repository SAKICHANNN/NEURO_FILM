# U5.R2U1 Hierarchical Colour Coupling Development Contract

Date: 2026-07-26

Status: **complete; pair fit passes but hidden operator fails**

## Parent and activation

U5.R2U0 establishes ColorFM's HCC as a distinct coupling prior, not an
observed correspondence method. U5.R2U1 may execute only after S4 has two
byte-identical reports and a final eligible branch. A shuffled-condition
negative that passes, or any structure/repeat failure, blocks activation.

This sequencing preserves the meaning of S3 and S4. HCC cannot be introduced
after seeing their numbers as an undocumented rescue, and no S3/S4 config,
seed, loss or result may change.

## Question

Can published hierarchical colour coupling construct pseudo-pairs that help
the validated bounded U5.R2O0 flow recover one hidden synthetic colour
operator across several corresponding content distributions?

The word *pseudo* is essential. HCC does not reveal true sample pairs. It
chooses a smooth, locality-preserving transport prior.

## Frozen observations

Four generated content conditions use the same preregistered support geometry
as S4 but entirely new development seeds `28400-28402`. Every condition/domain
contains three independently generated scenes with 128 samples each.

- source and target scenes, palettes, samples and pixels are disjoint;
- target samples alone pass through the hidden analytic style flow;
- condition IDs are generated truth, never inferred from appearance;
- the hidden grid and real sample correspondence are unavailable to fitting;
- confirmation seed `28403` remains untouched.

## Coupling controls

All methods fit one shared `4x4x4` U5.R2O0 flow:

1. random pairing inside the correct conditions;
2. pooled HCC after discarding condition IDs;
3. HCC separately inside the correct conditions;
4. HCC after fixed target-condition permutation `[1,3,0,2]`.

HCC recursively subtracts each subset's own mean, divides RGB offsets by sign
octant, and descends to depth three. Terminal subsets are paired by a frozen
random permutation without replacement up to the smaller size. An empty-tree
case falls back to the same frozen random rule.

The pair MSE is only an optimizer and diagnostic. It is not evidence of the
hidden operator.

## Required evidence

The primary correct-condition HCC method must jointly pass:

- hidden operator-grid output median/p90 error at most `.07/.10`;
- A/B independently observed/coupled operator disagreement at most `.04`;
- at least 25% median hidden-operator improvement over random, pooled and
  shuffled controls;
- shuffled HCC failure on hidden-operator or replicate stability;
- inherited style, cube, determinant, norm, inverse, coefficient, replay and
  byte-identical repeat gates.

Held-out correct-condition RFF-MMD is reported to show distribution fit, but
cannot substitute for hidden-operator recovery.

## Real-data stop

Even a complete synthetic pass does not open film pixels. Real use requires:

- evidence-eligible stock and rights;
- corresponding auxiliary conditions in neutral-digital and film domains;
- condition definitions independent of scene colour, content, source,
  scanner, uploader and geometry;
- held-out groups and zero leakage;
- a preregistered claim ceiling of `film-inspired/unpaired-evidence`.

The current pools do not meet those conditions. No ColorFM-L training,
semantic encoder, unconstrained velocity MLP or direct final-RGB network is
allowed.
