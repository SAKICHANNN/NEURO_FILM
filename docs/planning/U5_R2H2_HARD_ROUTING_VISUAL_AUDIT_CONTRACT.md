# U5.R2H2 exact hard-routing visual audit contract

Date frozen: 2026-07-23  
Node: `ULT > U5 > U5.R2 > U5.R2H2`  
Status: **frozen before panels or visual decisions**

## Question

Does the exact U5.R2H1 hard 1-NN policy remain free of confirmed severe
artifacts at full resolution, and does its finite set of actual interventions
look at least as coherent and intentionally stylized as the fixed global
density fallback under repeated blind autonomous review?

This is an A0 development diagnostic. It is not preference evidence,
confirmatory evidence, stock learning, or production validation.

## Immutable evidence

Hash-verify:

- the 41-source U4.1 A0 manifest;
- the U5.R2H1 formal report and its exact composite-view `hard_1nn`
  predictions;
- the frozen U5.R2H1 config, including the `0.5` assignment margin;
- the U5.R2B anchor56 report and render manifest;
- the U5.R2E1 cyan-shadow/warm-highlight density report and render manifest.

Class `0` is
`anchor56_chroma_margin4_challenger`; class `1` is
`cyan_shadow_warm_highlight_like__s50`.

No image is rendered, modified, refit, recoloured, resized for evaluation, or
used to change a prediction. Contact-sheet resizing is presentation only.

## Exact policy and complete coverage

- Apply the 37 immutable assigned H1 composite predictions exactly.
- The four composite-unassigned rows fall back to class `1`, the immutable
  H1 best-global density class.
- Inspect all 41 routed full-resolution files for the severe-artifact rubric.
- The global comparator is density for all 41 rows.
- The blind comparison population is all and only the ten rows routed to
  class `0`. These are every actual intervention relative to global density;
  no post-hoc visual shortlist is permitted.

Record source ID, H1 prediction or fallback, selected H1 training neighbour,
source/output hashes, exact routed path, global path, and whether the routed
choice agrees with the metric Oracle.

## Severe-artifact rubric

A confirmed severe failure includes face/limb/object/text corruption, broken
geometry, posterization or banding, large unintended clipping, colour blocks,
seams, repeated texture, or an unstable/local colour defect. Strong global
colour, intended contrast, grain, or halation is not itself a failure.

Any confirmed severe failure in a routed output rejects the H1 policy. The
comparison also records severe issues already present in the density global
output so an inherited issue is not falsely attributed to routing.

## Blind autonomous comparison

Build three deterministic presentation rounds over every intervention:

1. `full_fit`: source plus two full-frame candidates;
2. `detail_crop`: source plus deterministic central 60% crops;
3. `full_fit_reversed`: a separately seeded row order and side assignment.

The two candidates are labelled only `A` and `B`. Side assignment is derived
from SHA-256 of the frozen seed, round ID, and sample ID. The key is written
separately and must not be read while scoring a round.

For each row and round record:

- severe issue for A or B;
- style salience: A / B / tie;
- colour and content coherence: A / B / tie;
- overall non-severe direction: A / B / tie;
- a short observable reason.

These repeated rounds are robustness checks by one autonomous vision
evaluator, not independent raters.

## Frozen decision rule

Retain the exact policy as an A0 research challenger only if:

1. all 41 routed full-resolution outputs have zero confirmed severe failures;
2. no intervention introduces a severe failure absent from global density;
3. in every blind round, routed wins plus ties on overall direction are at
   least 7 of the 10 interventions;
4. the summed routed-minus-global overall score (`win=+1`, `tie=0`,
   `loss=-1`) is positive in at least two of three rounds.

Otherwise close this exact routing policy. No threshold, descriptor, feature,
operator bank, label, neighbour count, strength, or fallback may change after
viewing.

## Claim ceiling and branches

Maximum positive claim:

`A0 autonomous visual evidence that the exact source-only hard-1NN policy
selects between two already safe explicit looks without a newly observed
severe failure and with non-negative finite-bank visual coherence`.

A pass permits a separately frozen held-out-data design only. A failure
retains both global operators and closes this router. Neither branch permits
stock/mode/authenticity/calibration, human preference, population
generalization, production integration, or RGB-generating ML claims.

Ultimate remains active under every result.
