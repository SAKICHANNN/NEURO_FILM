# U5.R2I0 Sensitometry-Print Frontier Contract

**Frozen:** 2026-07-24  
**Node:** `ULT > U5 > U5.R2 > U5.R2I0`  
**Split:** B0 autonomous development

## Question

Does the fixed, unfitted U2.2B exposure-to-density-to-print composition create
a clearly stylized, non-basic and severe-artifact-clean photographic look on
the already frozen 9-gold/32-stress set?

U2.2B passed only synthetic numerical gates. This experiment is its first
real-image value/safety test. It is not stock learning: its parameters are
clean-room witnesses assembled before any real-film fitting, and current
pixel pools remain prohibited for fitting or training.

## Fixed bank and one changed variable

The U2.2B operator is immutable. Only a continuous, explicit linear-RGB
identity blend changes:

`output = input + strength * (U2.2B(input) - input)`

Strengths are frozen at `0.35, 0.50, 0.65, 0.80, 1.00`. These are one
operator direction, never five modes. There is no exposure adaptation,
per-image statistic, content route, local mask, clipping step, effect layer or
parameter fit.

The inherited R2B metrics/gates remain unchanged: gold median style Delta E76
at least `7.0`, median non-basic residual at least `4.9`, and worst-gold new
hard clipping at most `0.5%`. At most two surviving strengths advance, ordered
by non-basic residual, then style, then lower strength.

## Visual and severe gates

Only automatic survivors may enter three deterministic blind sheets against
safe-rich, anchor 56 and the retained R2E1 density challenger. All nine gold
images are required. Full-resolution review covers every shortlisted gold
render and explicitly checks ID11 for red speckle/posterization. Any confirmed
severe corruption rejects that strength regardless of style.

Autonomous vision may describe appeal and defects. It is not an owner vote,
independent human panel or population preference result.

## Branches

- **No automatic survivor:** close the U2.2B visual route; retain it only as a
  numerical primitive.
- **Automatic survivor but severe failure:** reject the failing strength and
  do not repair it after inspection.
- **Severe-clean survivor with weak visual value:** keep it as a control, not a
  champion.
- **Severe-clean, visibly valuable survivor:** retain one B0 Look
  Approximation challenger for later frozen cross-candidate evaluation; no
  renderer or stock promotion opens.

## Reproducibility and boundaries

Verify config/source/output hashes and dimensions; require two byte-identical
render manifests and deterministic reports. Generated images/reports remain
ignored. No generative AI, model training, film-pixel fitting, LSM, production
profile/schema/default change, authenticity or calibrated stock claim is
allowed.
