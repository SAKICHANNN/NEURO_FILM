# SF3.A3Y — Denmark Velvia 50 natural-ageing source readiness

## Role

This is a prospective zero-pixel source-readiness audit for the 2025 paper
`Evaluating the stability of colour slides: 30 years of natural ageing at the
National Museum of Denmark`. The physical study is materially stronger than
an after-only natural-photo pool: it includes Fujichrome Velvia ISO 50 and five
other reversal films, a colour checker, four replicates, untreated and three
fixer conditions, a freezer reference, multiple storage locations, and optical
density measurements in 1992 and 2024.

The leaf asks whether the exact measurements and sample identities are publicly
available with rights and roles sufficient for a separately frozen experiment.
It does not digitise article figures, request collection objects, or infer
unpublished spreadsheet rows.

## Frozen source and operations

1. Read the exact official OJS article page, its published PDF, the Royal
   Danish Academy Pure record, and the exact Crossref and DataCite DOI API
   endpoints.
2. Require the article title, author set, CC BY 4.0 article licence, exact PDF
   identity and the physical-design statements frozen in the config.
3. Record the OJS DOI string with its literal double slash and the Pure record's
   single-slash rendering. The mismatch is an observed catalogue fact, not a
   reason to rewrite either source.
4. Treat the paper's figures, tables and stated Microsoft Excel datasheet as
   evidence that measurements exist, not as a public machine-readable dataset.
5. Make zero requests for slide photographs, Excel data, climate logs, sample
   objects or any other unpublished research payload.

## Admission gates

Source audit requires an exact CC BY article/PDF identity and an explicit,
controlled Velvia 50 physical design. Scientific data admission separately
requires a public measurement payload, exact member manifest and checksums,
measurement-data licence, per-sample identifiers, and prospective development
and sealed-confirmation roles. Article-level CC BY does not silently license
unpublished collection objects or spreadsheets.

## Stop rules

- Do not recover values from plotted figures or PDF raster content.
- Do not contact authors, submit forms or request collection access in this
  leaf; outreach is governed by the separately authorised request packet.
- Do not treat the freezer and storage locations as ready train/test roles
  without row-level sample identities and a prospective protocol.
- Do not fit, render, score, tune gates or open candidate three.
- Failure closes only public source readiness; it is not a negative result about
  Velvia 50, natural ageing, or the authors' measurements.

## Claim ceiling

At most this leaf can establish that a high-value, controlled, longitudinal
Velvia 50 physical observation exists and that the article is openly licensed.
It cannot establish public data availability, reuse rights for unpublished
measurements or collection objects, stock response, calibration, learning
eligibility, three-stock evidence, candidate-three admission or product
capability.
