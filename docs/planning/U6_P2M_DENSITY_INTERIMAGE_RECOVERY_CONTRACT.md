# U6.P2M density-dependent interimage recovery contract

U6.P2M asks one narrow representation question: can an explicit, bounded
cross-layer development equation recover known synthetic interimage
suppression on held exposure groups when independent characteristic curves and
a fixed post-density affine map cannot?

The operator acts between layer exposure and developed dye density. Its six
nonnegative off-diagonal coefficients suppress one layer as a smooth function
of development activation in the other layers. The frozen per-layer curves are
known controls; this pilot fits only the six couplings. This isolates the new
mechanism from curve capacity and from the existing fixed dye-absorption
matrix.

The experiment uses no photographs and cannot touch the sealed AO9 71-pair
display-proxy pool. A pass is only generic physical-inspired representation
evidence. It cannot identify a stock, process, scanner, exposure condition,
push/pull state, or real digital-to-film operator. A later real fit would
require controlled single-layer and combined neutral sensitometry with rights,
roll/process groups, held groups, and independently recorded metadata.

The frozen executable contract is
`configs/u6_p2m_density_interimage_recovery_v1.json`. Relevant mechanism
precedents are Hanson and Horton (1952),
<https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSA.42.000663>, Wychorski (1976),
<https://repository.rit.edu/theses/4827/>, and Kodak's
<https://www.kodak.com/content/products-brochures/Film/Exploring-the-Color-Image.pdf>.
