# SF3.A3N — Parvec controlled-film source admission

## Question

Does Parvec's official 2025 *Every 35mm Film Color Science Explained — Part
1* project expose a rights-usable, machine-verifiable controlled observation
for the stock-first Portra 400 / Ektar 100 / Velvia 50 programme?

## Frozen source

- official project page: `https://www.parvec.es/35-mm-color-science-project`
- official video: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AVFTtE-8C94`
- official video identity: YouTube oEmbed title `Every 35mm Film Color Science
  Explained - Part 1`, author `PARVEC`
- required target chapters: `Kodak Ektar 100` and `Kodak Portra 400`
- required absent target chapter: `Fujifilm Velvia 50`

The project describes a methodical, consistent comparison and says its presets
derive from numerical hue/saturation/luminance measurements. It also says the
presets were manually refined, require a form/email handoff, and that release
of the underlying raw data/documentation is still being considered.

## Execution order and gates

1. Request only the official project HTML, official YouTube watch HTML and
   YouTube oEmbed JSON.
2. Verify the exact project/video identity and the controlled-workflow,
   numerical-data, manual-refinement, form/email and unreleased-documentation
   statements.
3. Verify target-stock chapter coverage without requesting video streams,
   thumbnails, presets, forms or email.
4. Require all three named stocks plus a public machine-readable numerical
   table, exact file/scene/roll manifest and checksums, explicit fitting and
   derived-parameter rights, and independent roll/source/scanner groups.
5. Any failed gate closes before image, preset, pixel, fit, render or score.

## Stop rules

- Do not submit the preset form, create an account, purchase, email or contact
  the author.
- Do not request video/audio streams, thumbnails, poster assets, presets or
  source photographs.
- Do not reconstruct measurements from rendered vectorscopes or transcript
  prose.
- Do not treat a free creative preset as raw measurement data, a stock response
  or permission to train, fit, redistribute or ship derived parameters.
- Do not substitute another Fujifilm stock for Velvia 50.

## Claim ceiling

At most this leaf can record a controlled, same-workflow physical-film
observation covering Portra 400 and Ektar 100. It cannot establish a complete
three-stock dataset, independent groups, reusable measurements, calibrated
stock response, fitting rights, product profile or release permission.
