# U5.R2BH0 — Fresh fixed-bank evaluator Oracle

## Question

Before fitting any content selector or router, does a fixed bank of existing
explicit, deterministic, severe-clean global looks provide a complete
per-image evaluator-Oracle advantage over the frozen AO6 t15/c35 champion on a
new population?

This is the missing non-censored product-value test identified by U6.P8BQ. It
does not reopen P8BP or any member's earlier promotion decision.

## Frozen bank

1. fixed B0;
2. fixed AO6 colour-only t15/c35;
3. fixed AP3 Ektachrome composition;
4. fixed AZ0 optical-density residual;
5. fixed native Standard at strength 1.0.

All implementations, parameters and provenance are hash-bound before rendering.
No arm may be refit, strength-tuned or repaired on this population.

## BH0S source gate

The source contract freezes twelve metadata-only selected raw.pixls.us CC0
rows, one per make, disjoint by repository ID, source hash and make/model from
all tracked RAW populations. Acquisition is capped at 256 MiB. At least nine
rows from nine makes must decode, with zero exact or dHash<=4 leakage, no more
than two near-empty/monochrome rows and zero confirmed severe source artifacts.
Failures are retained; replacement rows are forbidden.

Passing BH0S permits only fixed-bank rendering. It does not permit fitting,
training, stock claims or mode discovery.

## Complete Oracle gate

Every eligible source must receive a complete ranking of all five fixed arms in
each of three independently masked autonomous rounds. Missing pairwise order is
not imputed. Severe-artifact veto precedes preference. The gate and randomized
mappings are frozen before any rankings are read.

If the complete Oracle does not materially and stably beat AO6, selector and
router training close. If it does, only the simplest hard selector may open;
dense blending and direct RGB prediction remain forbidden. Results are
autonomous visual evidence, not population preference, stock response or
calibration.
