# SF3.A3Z — Hershenson-Allen Velvia archive source readiness

## Role

This is a prospective zero-media source-readiness audit of the official
Hershenson-Allen Archive. The site describes 41,987 digital movie-poster
images with corresponding 4-by-5-inch colour transparencies, photographed on
a fixed view-camera/vacuum-table setup with a colour calibration card. Fuji
Velvia 50 is explicitly named as one example of the professional film used.

That topology is materially different from a natural-photo label pool: it may
contain repeated fixed-setup physical captures of bounded graphic objects.
The leaf asks whether the public archive binds individual items to Velvia 50
and exposes rights, manifests, checksums and prospective roles sufficient for
a later experiment. It does not assume that an archive-level example film
applies to any individual item.

## Frozen sources and operations

1. Read only the official home, collection, exploration and prospectus pages,
   plus the site's `robots.txt` content signals.
2. Bind exact response identities and the fixed archive/workflow statements in
   the config.
3. Record the official prospectus link but do not fetch the PDF.
4. Treat "available digitally" as an availability statement, not as a public
   manifest, a neutral digital reference or permission to download images.
5. Make zero requests for poster images, transparencies, database rows,
   thumbnails, prospectus PDF bytes or any other media payload.

## Admission gates

The source audit requires exact official-page identities, the archive counts,
the fixed physical-capture workflow, the archive-level Velvia 50 statement,
the explicit all-rights-reserved footer, and current content signals. A later
pixel leaf additionally requires item-level Velvia identities, a public exact
manifest and checksums, an explicit image/data licence compatible with fitting
and derived parameters, scanner/process/group identities, an independent
same-object reference, and prospective development and sealed-confirmation
roles.

## Stop rules

- Do not infer an item's film from the archive-level "e.g." list.
- Do not scrape the queryable SQL database or enumerate image endpoints.
- Do not fetch the prospectus PDF, poster images, transparencies or thumbnails.
- Respect `ai-train=no` and the site's reference-only content signal.
- Do not fit, render, score, tune gates, open candidate three or claim that a
  poster image is a neutral digital reference.
- Failure closes public source readiness only; it is not a quality conclusion
  about the archive, the transparencies or Velvia 50.

## Claim ceiling

At most this leaf can establish the official archive-scale and fixed physical
capture description, including an archive-level Velvia 50 example. It cannot
establish item-level stock identity, public media/data availability, reusable
rights, neutral/film pairs, group-held roles, stock response, calibration,
learning eligibility, candidate-three admission or product capability.
