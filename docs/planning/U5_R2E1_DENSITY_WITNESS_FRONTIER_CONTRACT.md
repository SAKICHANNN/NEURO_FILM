Does any fixed bounded-strength witness from the U5.R2E0 clean-room
density-domain family produce an obviously stylized, non-basic result on the
existing U5.R2B inputs while retaining zero confirmed severe artifacts on all
nine gold images?

This is a B0 autonomous development experiment. It is not operator fitting,
stock identification, calibration, physical validation, population
preference or production promotion.

## Fixed candidate bank

Cross the five immutable descriptive witnesses with strengths:

`0.20`, `0.35`, `0.50`, `0.65`.

This yields exactly 20 candidates. The upper bound intentionally avoids
testing the full-strength mappings before any photographic safety evidence.
No exposure compensation, auto white balance, per-image normalization,
parameter fitting, post-result strength, local mask, spatial residual or
per-image route is allowed.

Ingress is decoded sRGB8 to D65 linear sRGB. The operator is applied in that
linear domain, clipped only for a defensive `1e-12` numerical tolerance,
encoded once to sRGB8 and saved as RGB PNG. It may not resample geometry or
add grain/effects.

## Integrity gates

- Verify the frozen-set, E0-config and R2B-config hashes before rendering.
- Verify all source hashes and require 820 candidate/sample records.
- Require exact dimensions, RGB decode, finite output and output hash.
- Run a second independent render and require byte-identical manifests and
  output hashes.
- Record code commit, config hash, candidate parameters and every file hash.

Any integrity failure invalidates E1 without interpreting visual quality.

## Automatic screen

Reuse the exact R2B implementation and definitions:

- gold median style Delta E76 from input `>= 7.0`;
- gold median residual Delta E76 after frozen joint
  EV/WB/contrast/saturation affine matching `>= 4.9`;
- worst gold new hard clipping `<= 0.5%`, endpoint epsilon `0.5/255`;
- report worst stress new hard clipping without using it to conceal a gold
  failure.

These are salience/non-basic/clipping diagnostics, not authenticity or
semantic-safety proof.

## Frozen shortlist

To keep visual review bounded and avoid strength-path pseudo-modes:

1. within each witness, retain only the passing strength with the greatest
   gold median style Delta E76; exact ties choose lower strength;
2. rank the five possible representatives by gold median non-basic residual,
   then style, then candidate ID;
3. admit at most the first three representatives.

This guarantees at most one strength per witness and at most three visual
candidates. It does not imply that different witnesses are real film modes.

## Blind visual and full-resolution veto

Only shortlisted candidates proceed. Build three deterministically shuffled
gold rounds containing:

- input;
- safe-rich;
- the retained margin-4 anchor56 B0 challenger;
- at most three anonymous E1 candidates.

The review order is:

1. severe artifact/glitch veto;
2. visible film-inspired style and non-basic palette/tone character;
3. preference relative to both comparators;
4. descriptive notes on skin, sky, foliage, reds, shadows and highlights.

Every shortlisted candidate must be inspected at full resolution on all nine
gold images. Explicitly preserve sample 11 as the neon-red
speckle/posterization regression. Also inspect smooth gradients, clipping,
banding, colour islands, text/logo and fine texture. One confirmed severe
gold failure vetoes the candidate; ambiguity does not promote.

Autonomous vision is B0 development evidence, not owner, human-panel or
population preference.

## Branches

- **No automatic survivor:** reject the E0 witness bank for current product
  value; preserve the primitive and open a new preregistered operator-family
  hypothesis or product leaf.
- **Automatic survivors, all visually severe:** reject those policies; never
  weaken the veto.
- **Safe but bland/basic:** retain as research negative evidence; safe-rich
  remains fallback.
- **Safe and visibly stylized but worse than anchor56:** retain only as a
  diversity/control candidate, not champion.
- **Safe and visibly stronger/preferred:** retain one B0 density challenger;
  do not change production default or claim stock truth.
- **Multiple visual ties:** choose lower strength, then simpler descriptive
  witness; do not average them.

Failure of the witnesses does not invalidate the E0 representation and never
stops the Ultimate Goal.
