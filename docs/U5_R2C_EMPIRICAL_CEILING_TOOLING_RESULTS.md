## Fail-closed evidence

The implementation rejects:

- drift from the exact `K=7` bank or comparator;
- identity counted as an eighth proposal;
- any authorization flag for pixels, recruitment, training, fitting, routing
  or production integration;
- duplicate or overlapping reviewer hashes across panels;
- forbidden U4/RF2.C0/ID11/U5.R2B origins in B1;
- parent-scene/source/creator/camera/roll/hash reuse across existing and
  proposed splits;
- incomplete all-scene policies;
- non-identity deployment when no candidate is eligible;
- incomplete or invalid tie-score outcomes.

The descriptive tie-score helper always returns
`one_sided_lower_bound=null` and `binding_decision_allowed=false`.
