## Blind visual and severe results

Both new strengths show a conspicuous green cast across unrelated scenes. It is stable and strong, but not competitive. This is direct negative evidence that a large non-basic residual is not sufficient evidence of a desirable film look, and that this strength path is not multiple modes.

All 18 shortlisted gold renders were inspected at original resolution. There is no confirmed face/geometry damage, seam, speckle, posterization or broken gradient. Both ID11 renders preserve continuous red and metallic transitions. The green cast is an aesthetic failure, not a severe glitch under the frozen ontology.

## Decision

- close the exact ungauged U2.2B visual route without tuning it;
- retain U2.2B as a numerical/negative control, not a product look;
- open one separately frozen, data-independent neutral-axis gauge hypothesis that inverts the operator's own neutral response before any new real-image result;
- preserve all stock/data stops, production defaults and the R2E1 challenger.

The next hypothesis is justified because U2.2B anchors layer density but does not constrain every display-domain neutral input to remain neutral. It may fail or remove the non-basic effect.
