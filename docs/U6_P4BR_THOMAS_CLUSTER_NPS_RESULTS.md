# U6.P4BR Thomas-cluster NPS result

U6.P4BR is a formal negative with a useful mechanism signal. It applied the
2023 IEEE Thomas-process spectral density to one material-shared Gaussian
particle footprint plus density-state cluster scale, multiplicity and one
positive amplitude. All fitting used the unchanged P4BP/P4BQ build bands.

Two reports are exact at SHA-256
`5422f6b24df8b2efe8688cf8f5040b20143475e20e0d0d66f5b8c4019edef5bf`
and stable ID
`d00539e216d21a0416df79cff4df4f5b296d7d66224f865268938bede7b9e288`.
The candidate beats the Gaussian control on four of five profiles and reduces
the worst confirmation log10 RMSE from `.92508` to `.51068` (ratio `.55203`).
Neopan's material median improves `42.69%`. However, the overall median is
`.41277` versus `.34271`, a `-20.45%` change, and Medical X-ray does not meet
its material gate. The exact one-level family therefore closes.

One pre-report execution used the complete frozen 160-generation optimizer
budget but stopped before confirmation scoring because SciPy labels exact
budget exhaustion unsuccessful. The evaluator was corrected to accept only
finite exact exhaustion of that same budget; no parameter, frequency, gate or
iteration changed, and both formal runs restarted from scratch.

The 4/5 and worst-tail gains justify clustering as a future hypothesis, but
adding more components on the same five source-fitted spectra would only chase
capacity. The next useful leaf is independent controlled uniform-field/NPS
evidence. No microscopic scale, stock or product claim opens.
