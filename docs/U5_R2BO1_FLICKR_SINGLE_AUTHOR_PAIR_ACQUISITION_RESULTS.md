# U5.R2BO1 paired derivative integrity

The bounded BO0 selection acquired all **106** 1024px JPEG derivatives
(23,962,120 bytes), preserving all **53** film/digital pairs across three
capture families. Every file passes hash, JPEG/RGB/single-frame decode and
metadata-dimension checks. There are no exact duplicate groups and no
cross-pair dHash pairs at distance 4 or below. A second complete audit is
byte-identical at SHA-256 `fe9b0697...a766d2`.

This opens preregistered geometric-registration feasibility only. It does not
authorize fitting: all pairs come from one author, their camera/lens systems
differ, and exact stock, exposure, process and scanner state remain unknown.
