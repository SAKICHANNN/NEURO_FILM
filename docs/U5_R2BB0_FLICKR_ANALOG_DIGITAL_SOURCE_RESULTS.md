# U5.R2BB0 Flickr Analog + Digital Source Result

The public Flickr group [Analog + Digital (pairs)](https://www.flickr.com/groups/lomoplusdigital/pool/)
is relevant because its rule asks for the same subject photographed once on
film and once digitally. U5.R2BB0 audited metadata only: signed-out pool pages
1-2 and the 136 unique photo pages visible from them. It did not request image
payloads.

Two complete audits are byte-identical at SHA-256
`446dd1c4193049b035e2524370f3a77742e4efa1fff93e2f7ea4255e93d9e767`.
The visible surface contains seven owners and six adjacent same-owner
film/digital candidates. None has derivative permission on both sides.
Seventy-seven rows use CC BY-NC 2.0, but all belong to one uploader; 62 expose
digital-camera metadata and zero expose film metadata. The other 59 rows are
All Rights Reserved.

**Decision:** close the current visible pool before pixel access. It proves
that community film/digital comparisons exist, but it cannot provide a
rights-eligible paired operator source. Adjacent group submissions also remain
weak candidates rather than verified pixel alignment. No fitting, training,
stock-response claim or product integration opens.

The exact decision is frozen in
`configs/u5_r2bb0_flickr_analog_digital_source_decision_v1.json`. A future
re-audit is justified only if additional pages become publicly visible or the
explicit-pair owners publish reusable licences.
